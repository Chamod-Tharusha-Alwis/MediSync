## Plain-language reading

- The projected value **is** the naive "repeat the last 72 hours" value, so there is no claim that it beats the naive baseline; the midpoint of the range is reported to show whether shifting the value by past changes would help (MAE 1.46 vs 1.44 on the synthetic series, 206.44 vs 182.95 on OpenDengue).
- The range is meant to hold the next value about 80% of the time (10th-90th percentile). Observed coverage: 87.7% on the synthetic series and 67.6% on OpenDengue weekly counts.
- The synthetic series only test the code: they were generated by this project (gamma-Poisson noise on a seasonal shape), so the numbers say nothing about real outbreaks.
- OpenDengue values are national weekly counts for Sri Lanka (455 weekly rows read; 14 short gaps (at most 3 weeks) interpolated by the existing study rule; source https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenDengue/master-repo/main/data/releases/V1.3.1/National_extract_V1_3_1.zip, SHA-256 c588335f25f2202e..., downloaded 2026-11-15). They are not MediSync consultations and not 72-hour district counts; one week stands in for one period.
- Origins with fewer than 8 past changes show "not enough history" and no range, exactly as on the admin screen.
