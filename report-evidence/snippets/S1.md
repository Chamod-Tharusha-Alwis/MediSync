**What it does:** For each district and disease, the detector skips groups with fewer than 5 distinct episodes in the last 7 complete days, then turns each day's count into a z-score against the mean and standard deviation of the previous 56 days. A signal is raised only when at least two consecutive days have z above 2.0, which is the rule the daily surveillance scan and the admin "Force System Scan" both run.
